## Test 1 - Portfolio loads with redesigned hero [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Description: Verifies the redesigned portfolio loads with the new hero and primary CTA.

Steps:

2. Wait for the page to fully load
3. Click "View Projects"

Expected: The hero headline "Practical systems for messy business workflows." is visible and the Projects section is reachable.
